## 3. Differential law of the seven-dimensional kernel projector

Let `P=I−B⁺B` be the orthogonal rank-seven projector on `ker B`, where `B⁺` is the stable Moore–Penrose inverse retaining only the three positive singular values. With constant rank,

\[
\dot P = -B^+\dot B P-P\dot B^T(B^+)^T.
\]

A seven-plane transported by the 10D tangent generator `J10` has instantaneous orthogonal projector rate

\[
\dot P_{\rm dyn}=(I-P)J_{10}P+P J_{10}^T(I-P).
\]

Define

\[
D=(\dot B+B J_{10})P.
\]

Then the exact constant-rank algebra gives

\[
\dot P-\dot P_{\rm dyn}=-\big(B^+D+(B^+D)^T\big),
\qquad B(\dot P-\dot P_{\rm dyn})=-D.
\]

These formulas separate the direct observer-change contribution `dotB P` and the tangent transport contribution `B J10 P`. Their sum is the instantaneous kernel-invariance defect, expressed per unit of the evolution coordinate `xi`.
