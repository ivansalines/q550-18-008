# v18.50p — Scientific decision rules

1. **Provenance first.** All source and output SHA-256 hashes of the v18.50o server sendback must pass before numerical claims can be transferred.
2. **Central-difference order.** A near-fourfold change in the weakest singular value when `h` doubles strongly suggests `O(h²)` truncation; it does **not** by itself prove true rank three. Require multi-step Richardson and preferably analytic/automatic differentiation before any structural rank claim.
3. **Separate right singular modes from full-state hidden directions.** The fourth right singular vector of a 4×10 `B` lies in its numerically inferred row space, whereas at least six other 10D directions lie in its null complement. A weak singular vector is not necessarily a distinguished mode of the physical tangent flow.
4. **Transport test conditionality.** `q′=Jq` transports the selected 10D tangent vector in the declared chart. Agreement with an instantaneous SVD right vector is sign- and basis-convention dependent; use absolute cosine and independently report stencil sensitivity.
5. **No universal metric.** The seed-scaled Euclidean 10D chart is inherited; no material kinetic metric has been derived. Covariance tests from v18.50n do not establish a unique material connection.
6. **Numerical provenance of tiny values.** Document all finite-difference steps, fourth-order differences, singular-value ratios, and relative errors. A large full-rank pseudo-inverse angle can be a numerical amplification of a truncation residual.
7. **No unjustified promotion.** Do not label any mode a physical flip, memory, kinetic normal mode, or material transport law without further independent derivation.
